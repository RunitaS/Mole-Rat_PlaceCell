##### IOs to the hippocampal CA1 region
- Entorhinal cortex
    - The entorhinal cortex is the major output region to the hippocampus with the layer II of the Medial (spatially selective grid cells) and Lateral (low spatial selctivity but other sensory contextual information) EC (MEC and LEC) providing inputs to DG and CA3 region of the hippocampus and the layer III of the EC, both MEC (grid + HD cells) and LEC (low spatial selctivity but other sensory contextual information) sending direct inputs to the CA1 region of the hippocampus
    - Additionally, the MEC projects to the proximal art of the CA1 (CA1 region closer to CA3) whereas the LEC proejcts to the distal part of the CA1 (CA1 region away from the CA3)

#### Rate code of place cells

- Spatially selective increase in firing rate is called the rate code of hippocampal place cells which encode information about the poition of the animal. 
- Rate code is sparse code as majority of the pyramidal cells are silent while the animal is active. They fire spontaneously during periods of anesthesia and slow wave sleep.
- Despite this, recordings from as low as 20-50 active pyramidal place cells is enough to decode the position of the animal emhasizing the redundancy of the spatial rate code of the hippocampus.

*What gives rise to spatial selectivity in CA1?*
- **Excitation and Inhibition balance**
- Excitatory inputs
    - CA3 vs MEC inputs shaping the spatial reps.
    - Inhibiting inputs from either, by chemical lesion or transgenic inactivation, make the fields in hippocampus wider with a decrease in peak firing rate.
- Inhibitory inputs
    - Sustained, fast, high firing rate. Most fire through out. 
    - ON/OFF place fields by some interneurons which increase or decrease their sustained firing rate at specific location might play a role in release of inhibition to place cells, allowing them to become active.
- Inhibitory inputs outweighs Excitatory inputs (at least 2x). 
- If excitation and inhibition were to be balanced equally, a slight offset in either inputs would lead to a shift in place code of CA1 place cells.
- Inhibition being the dominant force keeps most place cells silent, which fits the observed rate of active pyramidal cells in our recordings. Explains why CA1 spatial code is sparse.
- Synchronus excitatory inputs from CA3 and EC followed by feedforward inhibition enable a short time window of activity by CA1 pyramidal cells, which also explains why inhibiting inputs from either of the two areas causes the fields to fire widely with a decrease in peak firing rate.
- Hence place fields of place cells are a result of inputs from both excitatory and inhibitory inputs with inhibitory forces dominating excitation. These inputs have a precise temporal control over their firing to ensure the formation of stable spatial codes.

#### Theta and Phase code of place cells

##### Theta osccillations in the hippocampus
- Generation of theta from MS-DBB and MEC inputs
- Instrinsic theta due to recurrent network

##### Spike-LFP coupling in hippocampus
- Inputs from areas such as the MEC and MS along with intrinsic synchronus population activity within the CA1 region is known to give rise ot a promiment oscillation in peaking at 8Hz during periods of mobility known as the theta rhythm.
- In conjunction to the rate-code, the place cells of hippocampus make use of theta cycles as a clock by firing at specific phase of rhythmic theta. Hence, also called the temporal code of hippocampal place cells as it regulates the timing of spiking activity.
- Specifically, a place cell fires at a later phase of a given theta cycle on entry of the animal into its respective field. This preferred phase shifts to an earlier phase of theta each theta cycle. The phase preference at the center of the place field with peak firing rate usually reaches the trough of theta cycle, mid way between the begining and ending of a theta cycle. The spikes occur around the begining of the theta cycle by the time the animal exits the field. Hence, the specific phase of theta cycle where the place cell fires encodes information on the position relative to the begining, peak, and end of the place field.
