### physiology and senses
- underground life poses challenges for repsiration, thermoregulation, and snesory systems
- underground systems have thermal stability <read paper on temperature difference as zeitgeber>
- cylindrical body with a short tail which has abundance of sensory guard hair cells (also present throughout rest of the body: https://elib.tiho-hannover.de/receive/etd_mods_00001237), abdominal tesis, small eyes, missing pinneae with ears sensitive to lower sound freq ranges
- body temperature in range 33 to 35 degrees, low basal metabolic rate (0.76 +- 0.08 ml O2 g-1 h-1)(slghtly higher in reproductive compared to non-reproductive animals) diue to low thyroxine, higher than normal thermal conductance 
- Heart rate 200 +- 23, respiratory rate 74 +- 26 (can be as low as 36 +- 5 when sleeping) which is significantly lower than expected for similar sized mammals, due to hypercapnic, hypoxic, and high humidity sealed tunnel systems
- small eyes (2mm dia) but all structures are preserved. Rods dominate but are fewer and bigger, cones are abundant (more than diurnal) but mostly S-opsin receptors (blue lgiht) which is a side effect of low T4 required for low basal metabolic rate.
- acoustic hearing range from 0.6 to 1 kHz at 30dB which can go upto 4 kHz at hgiher intensties, might have an acoustic fovea in cochlea. THis range orresponds well with tunnel acoustics and its is ear morphology
